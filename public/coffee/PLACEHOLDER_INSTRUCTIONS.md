# ⚠️ IMAGE PLACEHOLDERS NEEDED

This file lists the required images for the coffee product sections.

## Required Images for `public/coffee/` directory:

### Product Cards (800x600px recommended):

1. **cappuccino.jpg**
   - Professional product photo of cappuccino
   - Top view or angled view
   - Rich foam with cocoa powder
   - Dark wooden surface background
   - Warm lighting

2. **latte.jpg**
   - Professional product photo of latte
   - Glass cup showing layers
   - Latte art (heart or rosetta)
   - Dark wooden surface background
   - Creamy tones

3. **mocha.jpg**
   - Professional product photo of mocha
   - Tall glass showing chocolate layers
   - Whipped cream on top
   - Chocolate drizzle
   - Dark background

### Feature Section (PNG with transparency preferred):

4. **cup-centered.png**
   - Single premium coffee cup
   - Centered, isolated product shot
   - Clean background (dark or transparent)
   - 800x800px recommended
   - Used as the rotating centerpiece in Features section

### Decorative Elements:

5. **bean.png**
   - Single roasted coffee bean
   - Transparent background
   - 100x100px
   - Used for floating animations

6. **splash-banner.jpg**
   - Wide coffee splash with coffee beans
   - 1920x400px (banner aspect ratio)
   - Dark, moody background
   - Dynamic, energetic feel
   - Used above product grid
